### Attention design target by class

The times below are the **unw_spoll2 profile**, not a claim about the latest incumbent. The ideal retains every key,
the cache updates, gate and normalization, and the existing precision contract. The 4.61 TB/s contiguous-stream plateau
is not an attainable assumption for random page-size-1 K/V. `cute_gather1/2` establish about 3.8 TB/s for that gather.
Critical-path time can be below standalone delivery time because its prefix overlaps qkv; those two clocks must not be
subtracted as if they were the same measurement.

For d1s/v1s the pure byte-plus-launch bound is about 1.6 us, while the current ledger's raised floor is 2.3 us; neither
proves that the remaining dependent edges can be removed. Page indices already issue before the PDL wait. The small
constants (inverse frequencies, scales, cache locations, and cooperative epoch) currently share the post-wait raw-qkv
copy group. Stamp first to establish whether that group is on the critical chain. An epoch move additionally needs its
reuse ordering checked. No speculative K/V read may precede validation of a speculated sequence length.

The first shared-memory probe (`receipts/evidence/dbt/dbt_attnstamp.json`, five cold full-layer graph replays on
7dac42e9) passed both reference checks. These are perturbed phase observations, not production timings. D1s has 32
active CTAs; its critical CTA was the last counter arrival in all five runs. V1s has 33: CTA32, owning seven new-token
keys [80001,80008) and no output rows, arrived last in all five; a different output CTA finished last. That CTA's
counter wait was 0.61–0.90 us. Raw/constants readiness after KV issue exposed only 0.032–0.352 us (d1s) and
0.032–0.096 us (v1s). Target the v1s new-KV tail before another early-KV move. Counter-return stamps precede the poll;
their excess above idle atomic latency remains unattributed, not measured contention. The probe has no spills or
new prefetch waterfalls; two existing single-active-lane D1s reduction loops gained an internal R2UR.

All structural changes preserve the release/arrival counts and prevent producer/consumer rejoins after register
donation (C7507). Codegen checks must use the **actual appended build flags**. Stamps are diagnostics, not speed
candidates; only the uninstrumented endpoint can qualify a change. New ncu samples answer a named unresolved edge or
pipe question and feed the timing model, rather than repeating a broad profile sweep.
